# Huda original therapy explainers

## Treatment

Audience: adult caregivers learning what common supports aim to do. Format:
portrait 9:16, 540 × 960; five independent, 48-second captioned films.
Identity: Huda's cream, navy, and teal palette, clean sans-serif lettering.
No real children, third-party video, music, clinical demonstration, or fabricated
clinician endorsement. Sound is intentionally absent; all explanation is visible
in captions and an offline transcript.

The promise is an introduction to possible goals and limitations, not a
treatment prescription or evidence of guaranteed benefit. Reference summaries
are independently written; source links and attribution appear in the app.

## Shot-by-shot script

The canonical screenplay for every film is the corresponding `segments` array in
`data/therapy-lessons.json`. All thirty shots are fully written there before
rendering. Each shot runs for eight seconds; headings and captions are burned
into the exported MP4, with the matching full text available in the app.
The identical film is encoded as VP9 WebM for web previews; native applications
bundle only the H.264 MP4s through platform-specific asset modules.

Each film has exactly this time structure:

1. 00:00–00:08: opening goal; diagram draws attention to the activity.
2. 00:08–00:16: relevant capability; diagram changes composition.
3. 00:16–00:24: practical context; labels and connections appear in sequence.
4. 00:24–00:32: individualized support; mechanism adapts or changes focus.
5. 00:32–00:40: evidence limitation or safeguard; comparison diagram.
6. 00:40–00:48: closing professional-care note and abbreviated references.

## Visual direction and variety

Communication shows a small picture board alongside a message arriving at a
partner. Conversation alternates the active speaker. Choice moves a highlighted
tile into a message strip. Access uses different routes to a single message.
Daily routine moves from one familiar task to the next. Steps reveals a
sequence; environment shows a busy setting becoming simpler. Balance shows two
different comfortable paths rather than a correct/incorrect child. Progress
tracks an individual goal, without fabricated outcome numbers. Play connects
two actors through a shared object. Together joins caregiver and professional
support to the same goal. Reference concludes with a source panel.

Persistent layers: one Huda masthead, the episode category, and the progress
track. Every shot owns its heading, caption, and diagram, so there are no doubled
layers on transitions. Captions remain readable at the start and end of each
shot. No flashing, strobing, or fast camera motion.

## Review

Content is grounded in CDC treatment descriptions, ASHA's AAC information,
NICE CG170, WHO's CST introduction, and the AAP clinical report cited in CDC's
references. No intervention is called a cure. Benefits are possibilities,
outcomes differ, and clinician assessment is encouraged. The behavioral episode
distinguishes ABA from the broader support category and emphasizes safety,
communication, assent, and quality of life.
